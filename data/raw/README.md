\# Raw Dataset



This folder contains source data collected from official sources.



Primary sources:

\- Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

\- BIS Standards / Know Your Standards

\- BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)

\- Official government notifications, orders and circulars



Rules:

\- Do not invent or infer missing values.

\- Preserve official terminology where possible.

\- Keep the original source URL for every record.

\- Record the source type and evidence.

\- Verify certification/mandatory claims against official notifications or schemes.
